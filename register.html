Three questions. Then you'll know — and you'll know why.

Buying the wrong rung is the expensive mistake — placement when you needed training, or training again when you needed placing. These are the three questions we ask on every enrolment call.

Question 1 of 3 · Skill
Do you already have a skill a global company would pay for?

We ask this first because it decides everything downstream. No filter, feed or placement engine can sell a skill that isn't there yet — and no amount of training helps someone who was already hireable and just badly represented. Be honest here; the answer costs you nothing but saves you a wrong purchase.

Question 2 of 3 · Timeline
When do you need the dollar income to start?

Timeline is not impatience — it's a real constraint that changes the correct answer. Someone who needs income in six weeks should not be starting a four-week build then applying alone; someone with six months should not be paying for speed they don't need.

Question 3 of 3 · Budget
What can you commit right now — without borrowing dangerously?

We ask this last, deliberately. Budget should narrow the options after skill and timeline have identified the right kind of help — never before. And the honest instruction stands: if the amount competes with rent or school fees, the correct answer is the next cohort, not a loan.
