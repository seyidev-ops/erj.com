9 Weeks · 90 Minutes a Day

Operations Manager → Remote Ops Coordinator · Abuja · 35
₦2.1M/yr (~$1,380)$38,400/year via Deel · 27.8× in dollar terms

Two young children, a demanding day job, and a hard ceiling of 90 minutes a day, four days a week — no weekends, no late nights, everything async. No international CV, no portfolio, no LinkedIn, no contacts abroad. In 9 weeks we rebuilt her CV around metrics, turned two real logistics wins (34% error reduction, 11% delivery-cost savings) into a Notion portfolio, built LinkedIn from scratch, and sent 14 personalised outreach messages — 6 replies, 3 interview processes, 1 offer from a US supply-chain SaaS company. She now works 7am–3pm WAT, async-first, no timezone penalty.

Not exceptional talent or luck — 90 minutes a day, applied consistently, in the right direction. The constraints don't disqualify you.

Employed & Discreet · 11 Weeks

Finance Manager · Lagos · 42
₦7.2M/yr (~$4,700)$71,000/year · contractor agreement

Twelve years of experience and a real fear that both worked against him: too senior to start over, too visible to be seen searching. Nothing could touch his LinkedIn, no recruiter could call his office, and two people at his company were connected to everyone in his industry. We ran the entire search outside the public channels — a private portfolio link instead of a rebuilt profile, direct outreach to hiring managers rather than job boards, interviews taken at 6:30am before work. He resigned only after the contract was signed, and served his full notice.

"Being 42 was not the obstacle I thought it was. Eleven years of closing books across three subsidiaries was the entire reason they wanted me."

No Degree · 6 Weeks
Ngozi
Retail Supervisor → Remote Customer Success · Enugu · 26
₦180,000/mo (~$115)$2,100/month · US SaaS company

An HND, no university degree, and a settled belief that she had "no real skill" — she just handled customers. Four years of handling customers is exactly the job global SaaS companies hire for and cannot fill. We named the work properly (Customer Success, not "retail"), evidenced it with the numbers she already had — 60+ daily interactions, a complaint-resolution rate she had never once written down — and spent one week learning Zendesk and Intercom well enough to speak about them honestly. Six weeks, 31 aimed applications, 4 interviews, 1 offer.

"Nobody asked about my degree. Every single person asked how I handle an angry customer at 11pm, and I had four years of answers."

Two Final-Round Rejections · Then the Offer
Tunde
Data Analyst · Lagos · 30
2 finals lost · 0 offers$46,800/year · employer of record

His CV worked. His aim worked. He reached the final round twice in three months and lost both — which meant the expensive leak was in the last twenty minutes, not the document everyone told him to rewrite again. Two things had been ending it: an answer about working unsupervised built entirely on adjectives, and a salary number he volunteered inside thirty seconds. We replaced the first with a description of his actual system — how he plans a week, where he writes things down, what he does when he is blocked and his manager is asleep — and rehearsed the money question until the silence after his range stopped frightening him. Third final round: offer, at the top of the band he named.

"I rewrote my CV four times for a problem that was never in my CV. The thing that was broken took two weeks of practice to fix."
